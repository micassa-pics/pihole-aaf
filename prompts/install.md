# Install or update pihole-aaf

1. Copy the block from the [shared prompt](manage.md) into your AI assistant.
2. Choose **Install or update**. Choose **Check only** for a read-only check.
3. Choose **The Full Monty** for an unattended install or update of all pages and optional features. Give the assistant the target and access details. It may briefly restart Pi-hole. For a custom install, choose your features. Address history and guarded saves need both DHCP and Query Log.
